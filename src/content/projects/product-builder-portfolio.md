This site is being shaped into a product-builder portfolio: projects first, writing second, with a content model that stays independent from the presentation layer.

## Principles

- Static-first and fast by default.
- Content stays editable through Pages CMS.
- Projects and writing are separate content collections.
- The visual theme can evolve without rewriting content.
